👋 Why this exists

Built by a fan who couldn't find anything

I'm Skylar — an engineer on Florida's Space Coast. Channel Brain started as a personal tool: I wanted to pull specific ideas out of a show I'd been following, and realized there was no way to search years of episodes for the moments that mattered.

So I built one. Then it became obvious the same problem sits inside every creator business: the archive is the most valuable asset the business owns, and it's the one thing nobody can search — not the team, not the audience.

Channel Brain is a done-for-you service, not a self-serve app. I set it up, I maintain it, and I work directly with early partners to shape what it becomes.
